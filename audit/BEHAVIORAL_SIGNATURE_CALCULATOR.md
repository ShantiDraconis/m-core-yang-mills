# Behavioral Signature Calculator Contract

Purpose: make this repository an auditable detector/calculator of mathematical behavior without assuming the universal point-care hypothesis.

For every V transition collect raw typed data before interpretation:
STATE; LOGIC; POSITION; TRANSFORM; BEHAVIOR; INVARIANTS;
INFORMATION_PRESERVED; INFORMATION_LOST; EXTREMA; CENTER;
ZERO_ZERO; OMEGA; COUNTEREXAMPLES; MINIMAL_HYPOTHESES;
DEPENDENCIES; PROVENANCE.

The state-2/center analysis must record:
- exact Data type;
- exact size/measure and codomain;
- independently defined middle predicate;
- reachability and extrema conditions;
- transition relation and well-founded/decrease evidence;
- terminal condition;
- candidate 2->1 theorem, as a target unless independently proved.

Cross-problem comparison fields:
EXACT_MATCH; NORMALIZED_MATCH; STRUCTURAL_MATCH; DYNAMICAL_MATCH;
DIVERGENCE; NOT_COMPARABLE; NOVEL_STRUCTURE.

Numeric comparisons are permitted only for compatible quantities or after a justified dimensionless normalization. Record raw value, normalized value, absolute delta, relative delta, residual and exact V/position. Never invent a percent-solved metric.

Detector outputs are conclusions, never premises:
PATTERN_DETECTED
INVARIANT_DETECTED
CENTER_SIGNATURE_DETECTED
0_0_SIGNATURE_DETECTED
OMEGA_BEHAVIOR_DETECTED
M_CORE_STRUCTURE_DETECTED
M_CORE_CROSSING_DETECTED
NOVEL_STRUCTURE_DETECTED
NO_0_0_SIGNATURE

0_0_SIGNATURE_DETECTED requires two concrete typed quantities and an explicit path on which both zero behaviors are established.

Audit status (GREEN/RED/FROZEN/CERTIFIED/OPEN_EXACT) is metadata, not mathematical behavior.

Any universal 2->1 / point-care law remains a hypothesis until separately proved in the mathematics of this repository. No sorry/admit/new axiom may promote it.
